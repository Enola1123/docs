Personal Center is the hub for managing the people and payees connected to your YiPex account. From here you can maintain your list of registered payment recipients, manage which users have access to your company account and what they can do, and switch between multiple companies if your email is linked to more than one.

## Recipient Management

The Recipient Management section lists all payees registered for outgoing payments from your account, including stablecoin recipients, fiat recipients, and withdrawal accounts. You add and edit recipients from within the relevant payment flows. For step-by-step instructions on adding each recipient type, see the **Sending Funds** guides.

## User Management

User Management shows every user linked to your current company. YiPex has three user roles, each with a distinct set of permissions.

| Role | Who assigns it | Permissions |
|---|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Automatically assigned to the user who completed company verification | Full permissions across the entire account. The role cannot be changed by the Super Admin themselves. |
| **Admin** | Added by the Super Admin | All permissions except enabling or disabling the outgoing payment review email. |
| **Receive Only** | Added by the Super Admin or an Admin | Collection and deposit (inbound) actions only. Cannot execute outgoing payments. |

**Managing Super Admin:** If you need to change the Super Admin email address, contact YiPex support — this cannot be done self-service.

**Managing Admin and Receive Only users:** The Super Admin can add, edit, disable, or delete Admin users. Both the Super Admin and Admins can add, edit, disable, or delete Receive Only users.

<Info>
  After you add a new user to your company, that user must self-register at the YiPex login page if they do not already have a YiPex account. Once their registration is complete, they automatically receive the permissions associated with the role you assigned. For the registration steps, see the **Registration** guide.
</Info>

## Switch Company

If your email address is linked to more than one company, you can switch between them without logging out. Use the **Switch Company** option in Personal Center or in the main navigation menu to move to a different company account instantly.
