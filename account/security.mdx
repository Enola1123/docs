YiPex uses multiple independent security layers to protect your account and your funds. At the authentication level, every login requires both your **login password** and a second factor — either a TOTP code via two-factor authentication (2FA) or an email verification code. For fund movements, a separate **transaction password** is required. Finally, an optional **outgoing payment review** step adds a one-time email code as a final gate before any payment leaves your account.

## Login Password

Your login password is the first credential you enter each time you access YiPex. After the password is verified, you must also complete 2FA or email verification before access is granted.

**To change your login password:**
Go to **Personal Center** → **Change Login Password** → enter your current password and your new password → complete the verification step to confirm the change.

<Tip>
  Change your login password regularly to reduce the risk of unauthorized access. If your account was created by a YiPex operator and you were given a default password, change it immediately the first time you log in.
</Tip>

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Until you configure 2FA, logging in requires an email verification code as the second factor. Setting up 2FA replaces the email code with a time-based one-time password (TOTP) generated by an authenticator app — a more reliable option that works even when your email is inaccessible.

YiPex recommends **Google Authenticator**, but any standard TOTP-compatible app is supported.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the 2FA setup page">
    Go to **Personal Center** → **Set Up 2FA**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open your authenticator app and scan the QR code displayed on the page to link it to your YiPex account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify setup">
    Enter the 6-digit code shown in your authenticator app to confirm the link is working. Once verified, 2FA is active on your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Store your 2FA backup codes in a safe place immediately after setup. If you ever lose access to your authenticator app and have no backup codes, you may be completely locked out of your account.
</Warning>

## Transaction Password

The transaction password is a separate credential required for every outgoing payment operation. Unlike the login password, the transaction password is **shared by all users within the same company** — it belongs to the company, not to an individual user.

Set your transaction password as soon as your company verification is approved so that outgoing payments are never blocked.

**To change your transaction password:**
Go to **Personal Center** → **Change Transaction Password** → enter your current transaction password and your new one → complete verification to confirm.

<Warning>
  If you forget your transaction password, there is no self-service reset option. You must contact YiPex support and complete their identity verification process before the password can be reset. Keep your transaction password stored securely and share it only with trusted colleagues who need to authorize payments.
</Warning>

## Outgoing Payment Review

The outgoing payment review is an optional additional approval step for all outgoing payments. When enabled, after a user successfully enters the transaction password, YiPex sends a one-time verification code to the **Super Admin's email address**. The payment is processed only after the correct code is entered.

This feature is **disabled by default**. The Super Admin can enable or disable it at any time from Personal Center.

<Note>
  Only the **Super Admin** role can enable or disable the outgoing payment review. Admins and Receive Only users cannot change this setting.
</Note>
