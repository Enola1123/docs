Before sending funds to or receiving funds from a blockchain address, use YiPex's address risk check to detect potentially contaminated, sanctioned, or otherwise high-risk addresses. Catching a risky address before a transaction saves you from failed deposits, frozen funds, and lengthy remediation processes. The verification tool is accessible from the **top-right area of the dashboard**.

## Free Tier

The free tier gives you a quick risk signal for any address.

1. Open the address verification tool and fill in the required fields (wallet address and network details).
2. Click **Check Result**.
3. YiPex returns a **risk score** for the address.

<Info>
  The free check provides a risk score only. It is not a guarantee that a transaction to or from that address will succeed. Use it as a first-pass screening tool rather than a definitive clearance.
</Info>

## Paid Tier

The paid tier delivers a full risk assessment and a transaction guarantee for low-risk addresses.

1. Enter the wallet address (no additional fields required).
2. Click **Check Result**.
3. YiPex returns a **risk level** (for example: low risk, medium risk, or high risk). If the address carries any risk, YiPex also provides the specific reasons for the risk rating.

If the address is rated **low risk**, YiPex guarantees\* that the transaction will complete successfully.

New accounts receive a complimentary trial quota when they join. Once the trial quota is exhausted, you can purchase additional verification credits as needed.

<Info>
  \*The low-risk guarantee is conditional. After verification confirms an address as low risk, that address must **not** subsequently receive funds from unverified or unknown sources. If the address becomes contaminated after your verification check, the guarantee is void and YiPex does not cover any resulting transaction failures or losses.
</Info>

## Important Guidance

<Warning>
  Once an address has passed risk verification and you conduct a transaction, that address must not receive funds from any unverified source. Subsequent contamination — even from a single inbound transfer from an unknown wallet — can cause your transactions to fail. YiPex does not cover losses arising from post-verification address contamination.
</Warning>

For large inbound transfers, we recommend requesting a **USD 1–10 test transfer** first. Confirm the test amount arrives successfully before sending the full transaction amount. This is especially important when transacting with a new counterparty for the first time.
