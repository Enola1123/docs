Logging in to YiPex takes just a few seconds. Because YiPex allows one email address to be linked to multiple company accounts, the login flow includes an optional company-selection step so you always land in the right workspace.

<Note>
  YiPex provides its global trade settlement services exclusively to businesses registered outside mainland China and the United States.
</Note>

## Accessing the Login Page

Open your browser and go to **[https://app.yipex.tech/merchant/#/login](https://app.yipex.tech/merchant/#/login)**. Enter your registered email address and password, then proceed.

## Selecting a Company

YiPex supports linking one email address to multiple company profiles. Depending on how many companies are associated with your email, the platform will respond differently after you authenticate.

**If multiple companies are linked to your email**, YiPex displays a company selector screen. From here you have two options:

- **Select a company** — click any company in the list to enter its dashboard directly.
- **"Do not log in with any of the above"** — click this option at the bottom of the list to enter the platform without choosing an existing company. This takes you to the enterprise verification flow, where you can register a new entity.

**If only one company is linked to your email, or if no company is linked yet**, YiPex skips the selector screen and takes you directly into the platform.

## After Login

Once you select a company (or the selector is skipped), you land on the main YiPex dashboard where you can access collections, payments, wallets, and all other platform features.

<Tip>
  If you need to switch between company accounts after logging in, use the **Switch Company** option inside the platform — you don't need to log out and log back in.
</Tip>
