YiPex offers two ways to create an account: a fully self-service flow you can complete in minutes from any browser, and an operator-assisted path for businesses that prefer to submit company materials offline. Both routes give you access to YiPex's Web3-powered stablecoin settlement platform for cross-border trade.

<Note>
  YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities.
</Note>

## Self-Service Registration

Register directly at [app.yipex.tech/merchant/#/login](https://app.yipex.tech/merchant/#/login) without any prior contact with YiPex.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Go to [https://app.yipex.tech/merchant/#/login](https://app.yipex.tech/merchant/#/login) and click **Register** to open the registration dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details and submit">
    Type your business email address, then check the boxes to accept the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**. If the system prompts you for a CAPTCHA, complete it to confirm you are not a bot. Click **Register Now** to submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox for a confirmation email">
    YiPex will send a confirmation email to your registered address. Check your inbox — and your spam or junk folder — since automated emails can occasionally be filtered by mail providers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your login password">
    Follow the link in the confirmation email to reach the password-setup page. Choose a strong password and save it securely. Once you submit, your YiPex account is active and ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Operator-Assisted Registration

If you prefer to have the YiPex team handle registration on your behalf, you can submit your company materials offline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit your company materials to the YiPex team">
    Provide your company registration documents — including the email address you want to use for login — to the YiPex team through the agreed offline channel. The team will guide you on exactly which documents are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your login credentials by email">
    Once the YiPex team completes registration, they will send a notification email to your login address. Check your inbox and spam folder. The email includes the login URL and your **default password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and change your default password immediately">
    Use the credentials in the email to log in for the first time, then change your password straight away before taking any other action.

    <Warning>
      The default password is a temporary credential shared through email and must be changed on first login. Leaving it unchanged puts your account and any future funds at risk. Navigate to **Personal Center → Change Login Password** immediately after your first login.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you register through the operator-assisted path, the YiPex team completes enterprise verification on your behalf as part of the onboarding process. You do **not** need to submit a separate verification application after registration.
</Note>
