Enterprise verification is the KYC step that unlocks the full YiPex platform. Until your company is verified, you can explore the dashboard and pay collection requests issued by other verified YiPex users, but you cannot initiate your own collection requests. Completing verification removes this restriction and gives your business access to all settlement features.

<Info>
  YiPex provides its global trade settlement services exclusively to businesses registered outside mainland China and the United States.
</Info>

## When Verification Is Required

You will be prompted to complete enterprise verification in either of these situations:

- **No company is linked to your email** — for example, you have just self-registered and have not yet submitted a company profile.
- **You chose "Do not log in with any of the above" at the login company selector** — selecting this option enters the platform without an active company and triggers the verification prompt.

In both cases, the dashboard will display a banner prompting you to start verification.

## Verification Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Go to Verification' on the dashboard prompt">
    After logging in, locate the verification banner on the dashboard and click the **Go to Verification** button. This opens the enterprise verification form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your complete company documents">
    Follow the guided form to provide your company information and upload all required supporting documents. Ensure every field is accurate and every document is legible — incomplete submissions extend the review timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for YiPex to complete its review">
    After you submit, the YiPex compliance team will review your materials. Reviews are typically completed within **2–3 business days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your registered email for the review result">
    YiPex will send the outcome — approval or a request for additional materials — to your registered email address. Monitor both your inbox and your spam or junk folder, as automated emails can occasionally be filtered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supplement materials if requested">
    If the review team finds that any documents are missing or incomplete, you will receive an email with specific instructions. Follow those instructions to submit the requested materials so the review can proceed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Unverified accounts can view the platform and **pay** collection requests created by other verified YiPex users, but cannot **initiate** their own collection requests. Full collection functionality becomes available only after your enterprise verification is approved.
</Note>
