YiPex is a Web3-powered settlement platform built for international trade businesses. Whether you need to collect stablecoin payments from overseas buyers, receive fiat via bank transfer, convert currencies, or pay out suppliers globally — YiPex provides a single, compliant platform to manage it all.

<Note>
YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities. Screenshots in this guide may show test environment data; actual product details may vary.
</Note>

## How It Works

YiPex separates your funds into two wallets:

- **Crypto Wallet** — holds USDT and USDC received via blockchain. You can pay out in stablecoins, convert to fiat, or use the express payout service.
- **Fiat Wallet** — holds USD, CNH, and other fiat currencies received via bank transfer or converted from crypto. You can make global payments, withdraw, or exchange currencies.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.yipex.tech](https://app.yipex.tech/merchant/#/login) using your business email, or have a YiPex representative register on your behalf. See [Registration](/getting-started/registration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Enterprise Verification">
    Submit your company documents for KYC review. Approval typically takes 2–3 business days. See [Verification](/getting-started/verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Wallets">
    Apply for fiat receiving accounts and add recipient addresses for outgoing payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Transacting">
    Issue collection requests to buyers, top up via crypto deposit, pay suppliers, and exchange currencies — all from your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
