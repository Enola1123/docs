Crypto Collection lets you accept USDT or USDC from buyers on any blockchain network supported by YiPex. Once a payment is confirmed on-chain and passes YiPex's automated risk scan, funds land in your crypto wallet within minutes — no bank intermediaries required.

## Prerequisites

Your enterprise verification must be approved before you can initiate a crypto collection request. Unverified accounts can pay collection requests from verified users, but cannot generate their own receiving addresses.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Collect dialog">
    Click **Collect** on your YiPex dashboard. In the dialog that appears, select **Crypto Collection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose currency and network">
    Select the receiving currency (**USDT** or **USDC**) and the blockchain network you want to receive on.

    <Warning>
      You must select the exact same network the payer will use to send funds. Sending to a mismatched network (for example, sending TRC-20 USDT to an ERC-20 address) will result in permanent asset loss. Always confirm the network with your payer before proceeding.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the payer's email (optional)">
    Optionally enter the payer's email address. If provided, YiPex will automatically send the receiving address to that address on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm collection info">
    Click **Next – Confirm Collection Info**, review the details on the confirmation screen, and submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the receiving address">
    YiPex generates a one-time receiving address. Copy it and share it with your payer directly, or — if you provided their email in step 3 — confirm that YiPex has already emailed it to them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the payer to transfer before expiry">
    Request that your payer completes the transfer before the address expiry shown on screen. Transfers that arrive after the expiry window will still be credited in the vast majority of cases, but sending on time is always preferable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security and AML

YiPex automatically scans the sender's blockchain address for risk signals after every incoming transfer.

- **If the address is clean:** funds arrive in your crypto wallet within **5–10 minutes**, and YiPex sends an email notification. You can also check the status in **Recent Transactions** or **Crypto Wallet Statement** on the dashboard.
- **If funds have not arrived after 15 minutes:** contact the YiPex support team for assistance.

<Warning>
  If funds arrive from an address that fails the risk scan, YiPex will return them to the sender at your request — but a **3% service fee** will be deducted from the returned amount. To protect yourself:

  - Request the payer's sending address before initiating the collection and run a risk check via the **Blockchain Address Verification** tool.
  - Alternatively, ask the payer to send a small test amount (USD 1–10) first. Confirm it arrives before requesting the full transfer.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Once an address has passed a risk check or successfully completed a test transfer, that address must not receive funds from unknown or unverified sources afterward. Any subsequent transfer from a risky or unknown address can contaminate the previously clean address, causing future incoming transfers to be blocked or rejected.
</Tip>
