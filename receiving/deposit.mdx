The Deposit (top-up) feature is designed for the **buyer role** in B2B international trade — specifically to handle situations where a retail or end-buyer cannot provide a formal collection document. Rather than raising a collection request, buyers can deposit funds directly into their YiPex crypto wallet, using the trade documentation from their B2B orders (contracts, bills of lading, purchase orders) as the KYT/AML evidence for the incoming funds.

<Note>
  YiPex provides trade financial services exclusively to entities that are **not** incorporated in mainland China or the United States. Screenshots in this guide are from the YiPex test environment; actual product features and data may differ.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Deposit dialog">
    There are two ways to start:
    - Click **Deposit** on your YiPex dashboard homepage, **or**
    - Click **Deposit** directly under the relevant stablecoin balance (USDT or USDC) in your crypto wallet.

    Both entry points open the same Deposit dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select currency and network">
    In the dialog, choose the **currency** (USDT or USDC) and the **blockchain network** you will be sending from (for example, ERC-20, TRC-20, or another supported network). Make sure the network you select matches the network of your external wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate your deposit address">
    Click **Generate Deposit Address**. YiPex will display the deposit address for the selected currency and network.

    <Warning>
      ETH (ERC-20) and TRX (TRC-20) deposit addresses are designed to remain stable as long as they have not been contaminated, but **they can change**. Always generate a fresh deposit address immediately before each deposit to guarantee your funds will arrive. Never reuse an address from a previous session without regenerating it first.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send funds from your external wallet">
    Open your external wallet and initiate a transfer to the deposit address YiPex provided. Ensure the amount, currency, and network all match exactly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Your Deposit

YiPex automatically scans the sender's blockchain address for risk signals after every deposit.

- **If the address is clean:** funds arrive in your YiPex wallet within **5–10 minutes**, and YiPex sends an email notification. You can also check the status in **Recent Transactions** or **Crypto Wallet Statement** on the dashboard.
- **If funds have not arrived after 15 minutes:** contact the YiPex support team for assistance.

To protect yourself from losses caused by transactions with risky addresses:

- Use the **Blockchain Address Verification** tool to check your own sending address for risk before depositing.
- Alternatively, send a small test amount (USD 1–10) first, confirm it arrives, and then send the full amount.

If a deposit arrives from an address that fails the risk scan, YiPex will return the funds at your request, but will deduct a **3% service fee** from the returned amount.

<Tip>
  Once your sending address has passed a risk check or completed a successful test deposit, do not allow that address to receive funds from unknown or unverified sources. Any subsequent transfer from a risky address will contaminate the previously clean address, and future deposits from it may be blocked or rejected.
</Tip>
