YiPex gives you two distinct ways to receive funds from international buyers: **Crypto Collection**, which settles in USDT or USDC on-chain, and **Fiat Collection**, which brings USD or other currencies in through traditional bank transfer. Both modes are accessible from the same dashboard, so you can choose whichever method best suits each transaction or counterparty.

<Note>
  YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities.
</Note>

## Crypto vs Fiat Collection

| | Crypto Collection | Fiat Collection |
|---|---|---|
| **Currency** | USDT, USDC | USD and other fiat currencies |
| **Rails** | Blockchain network | Bank transfer (SWIFT / local) |
| **Arrival time** | 5–10 minutes (after risk scan) | 1–3 business days |
| **Prerequisites** | Enterprise verification | Enterprise verification + approved payment account |
| **Reusable address** | New address per request | Permanent account details |
| **AML scan** | Automatic sender address scan | Standard bank compliance |

<Note>
  Unverified accounts can pay collection requests issued by verified YiPex users, but they cannot initiate their own collection requests until enterprise verification is complete. Complete your verification first so you can collect on your own terms.
</Note>
