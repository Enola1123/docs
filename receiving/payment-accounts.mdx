Before you can issue a fiat collection request, you need at least one approved payment account. A payment account acts as the set of bank details — account holder name, bank name, SWIFT code, account number, currency, and jurisdiction — that YiPex presents to your payers when they initiate a bank transfer to you. You can hold multiple accounts in different currencies or regions to serve different payers.

<Note>
  YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities.
</Note>

## Account Types

- **Global Receiving Account** — A USD-denominated account that accepts international SWIFT wire transfers from payers anywhere in the world.
- **Local Receiving Account** — An account denominated in a local currency, settled through in-country payment rails in the payer's country or region. Local accounts typically offer faster settlement and lower fees compared to SWIFT for supported regions.

## Applying for a Payment Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Payment Accounts page">
    Navigate to the **Payment Accounts** page from the main menu, then click **Apply for Payment Account** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select account type">
    In the application window, choose either **Global Receiving Account** or **Local Receiving Account** depending on which type suits your payers and trade flows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in account and trade background information">
    Complete all required account information fields and provide your trade background details as prompted by the form. When everything looks correct, click **Submit Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for review">
    Your application enters review status immediately after submission. You can check the current status at any time by returning to the **Payment Accounts** page and viewing the account list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval — account becomes Active">
    Once your application is approved, the account status changes to **Active**. You can now:
    - Click the account card to view full account details.
    - Copy the account information to share with payers.
    - Select this account when issuing a fiat collection request from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rejection — edit and resubmit">
    If your application is rejected, the card will display the reason for rejection. Click **Re-apply** in the bottom-right corner of the card to open the form with your previous entries pre-filled. Correct the information as indicated and submit again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Your Accounts

From the **Payment Accounts** page you can:

- **Filter** accounts by type (Global or Local), status (Under Review, Active, Rejected), or currency to quickly find what you need.
- **Copy** account info directly from the account card for quick sharing with payers.
- **Resubmit** rejected applications via the **Re-apply** button on each rejected card.

<Tip>
  Apply for your payment account well before you need to issue your first fiat collection request. Account reviews take time, and you cannot select an account in a collection request until its status is **Active**. Getting your account approved in advance means you are ready to collect the moment a deal closes.
</Tip>
