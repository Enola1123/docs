Crypto Express Payout lets eligible merchants convert USDT or USDC and deliver the equivalent fiat amount directly to a bank account — all in a single, streamlined operation. There is no need to first move funds into your fiat wallet and then initiate a separate payment; the conversion and payout happen together, with a live rate locked at confirmation time.

<Note>
Crypto Express Payout is available **only to merchants who have been granted access by YiPex**. If your account has this feature enabled, a **Crypto Express Payout** entry button will appear on your dashboard. Contact the YiPex team if you wish to apply for access.
</Note>

<Note>
YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities. Screenshots in this guide reflect test-environment data; always refer to the live product for exact details.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

You must have at least one **Fiat Recipient** or **Withdrawal Account** saved in Recipient Management before you can initiate a Crypto Express Payout.

## Add a Recipient

Crypto Express Payout supports two recipient types, depending on who you are paying:

- **Fiat Recipient** — use this for a third-party supplier or partner bank account. See [Global Payment → Add a Fiat Recipient](/sending/global-payment#add-a-fiat-recipient) for setup steps.
- **Withdrawal Account** — use this for your own company's same-name bank account. See [Withdrawal → Add a Withdrawal Account](/sending/withdrawal#add-a-withdrawal-account) for setup steps.

<Note>
YiPex does **not** review fiat recipients or withdrawal accounts at the time of submission. Review occurs when you initiate the payout — not at setup.
</Note>

## Make a Crypto Express Payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Crypto Express Payout">
    Click **Crypto Express Payout** on your dashboard to open the payout window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select currencies">
    Choose the **order currency** (the stablecoin you are paying from — USDT or USDC) and the **target fiat currency** you want the recipient to receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payout details">
    Enter the **payout amount** and select the **target bank account** from your saved recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and continue">
    Verify that all details — currencies, amount, and destination account — are correct, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your transaction password">
    Enter your **transaction password** to authenticate the operation. If your super-admin has enabled Outgoing Review, also enter the verification code sent to the super-admin email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock the rate">
    YiPex displays a live exchange rate with a countdown timer. Review the locked rate and the final settlement amounts carefully before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm to initiate">
    Click **Confirm** before the countdown expires to initiate the payout at the locked rate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

YiPex sends an email notification to the relevant addresses once the payout is initiated. You can also monitor the status in **Recent Transactions** on the dashboard or under **Crypto Wallet Ledger**.

<Info>
Fees for Crypto Express Payout vary by network and are deducted from your crypto wallet balance. Review the fee shown during the quote step before confirming.
</Info>
