YiPex offers four outgoing payment methods designed to cover the full range of cross-border trade scenarios. Whether you are settling a stablecoin invoice, paying a foreign supplier via SWIFT, repatriating funds to your own company bank account, or converting crypto directly to fiat in a single step, YiPex has a path that fits your workflow.

<Note>
YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities. Screenshots in this guide reflect test-environment data; always refer to the live product for exact details.
</Note>

## Comparison of Payment Methods

| Method | Source Wallet | Destination | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stablecoin Payment** | Crypto wallet | External blockchain address | Recipient address must be pre-approved and show "Available" status |
| **Global Payment** | Fiat wallet | Any bank worldwide via SWIFT or local network | Supports USD and CNH; local network available in 22 countries |
| **Withdrawal** | Fiat wallet | Your company's own same-name bank account | No trade documentation required at account setup |
| **Crypto Express Payout** | Crypto wallet | Fiat bank account (direct settlement) | Eligible merchants only; rate locked at confirmation |

## Security Requirements for All Outgoing Payments

<Note>
Every outgoing payment on YiPex requires you to enter your **transaction password** before it can be submitted. The transaction password is shared across all users of the same company — set it promptly after your company completes verification and keep it secure. If your super-admin has enabled the **Outgoing Review** feature, YiPex will also send a one-time verification code to the super-admin email address; you must enter this code to complete the payment. Contact your super-admin if you do not receive the code.
</Note>
