YiPex offers four outgoing payment methods designed to cover the full range of cross-border trade scenarios. Whether you are settling a stablecoin invoice, paying a foreign supplier via SWIFT, repatriating funds to your own company bank account, or converting crypto directly to fiat in a single step, YiPex has a path that fits your workflow.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stablecoin Payment" icon="coins" href="/sending/stablecoin-payment">
    Pay USDT or USDC to any approved external blockchain address from your crypto wallet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Global Payment" icon="globe" href="/sending/global-payment">
    Send USD or CNH to any bank worldwide via SWIFT or local payment networks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Withdrawal" icon="building-columns" href="/sending/withdrawal">
    Withdraw USD or CNH directly to your company's own same-name bank account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crypto Express Payout" icon="bolt" href="/sending/crypto-express-payout">
    Convert and pay out USDT/USDC to a fiat bank account in one seamless operation (eligible merchants only).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Comparison of Payment Methods

| Method | Source Wallet | Destination | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stablecoin Payment** | Crypto wallet | External blockchain address | Recipient address must be pre-approved |
| **Global Payment** | Fiat wallet | Any bank worldwide via SWIFT or local network | Supports USD and CNH |
| **Withdrawal** | Fiat wallet | Your company's own same-name bank account | No trade documentation required at account setup |
| **Crypto Express Payout** | Crypto wallet | Fiat bank account (direct settlement) | Eligible merchants only |

## Security Requirements for All Outgoing Payments

<Note>
Every outgoing payment on YiPex requires you to enter your **transaction password** before it can be submitted. If your super-admin has enabled the **Outgoing Review** feature, YiPex will also send a one-time review code to the super-admin email address; you must enter this code to complete the payment. Contact your super-admin if you do not receive the code.
</Note>
