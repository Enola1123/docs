Stablecoin Payment lets you send USDT or USDC to any external blockchain address directly from your YiPex crypto wallet. It is the fastest way to settle crypto invoices or pay suppliers who accept on-chain stablecoin transfers. Before you can initiate a payment, you must add the recipient's address and have it cleared by YiPex's risk-screening system.

## Prerequisites

You must have at least one approved **Crypto Recipient** (also called a Stablecoin Recipient) saved in Recipient Management before you can make a stablecoin payment.

## Add a Stablecoin Recipient

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Recipient Management">
    Click **Personal Center** in the navigation menu, then select **Recipient Management** from the sub-menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new recipient">
    Click **New Recipient** in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the recipient type">
    On the New Recipient page, choose **Crypto Recipient**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter recipient details">
    Fill in the following fields:
    - **Currency** — select USDT or USDC
    - **Network** — select the blockchain network (e.g., TRC20, ERC20)
    - **Address** — paste the recipient's wallet address
    - **Label** — enter a recognisable name for this address

    Double-check all details, then click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for risk screening">
    YiPex automatically scans the address for risk. This takes **5–10 minutes**. Once the check passes, the address status changes to **Available** and you can use it for payments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If the address status has not updated after **15 minutes**, contact the YiPex support team for assistance.
</Note>

## Make a Stablecoin Payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment panel">
    Click **Pay** on the dashboard, or click **Pay** beneath the USDT or USDC balance in your crypto wallet. This opens the payment drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select wallet and method">
    Set **Payment Wallet** to **Crypto Wallet** and **Payment Method** to **Stablecoin Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the recipient">
    Choose the recipient you added earlier from the drop-down list. The currency, network, and address are filled in automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the address">
    Carefully confirm that the auto-filled address, currency, and network are all correct before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type the payment amount, then click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Enter your transaction password when prompted. If your super-admin has enabled Outgoing Review, also enter the review code sent to the super-admin email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

YiPex sends an email confirmation to the relevant addresses once the payment is initiated. You can also track the payment in **Recent Transactions** on the dashboard or under **Crypto Wallet Ledger**.

<Info>
Fees for stablecoin payments vary by blockchain network. The applicable fee is deducted directly from your crypto wallet balance in addition to the payment amount.
</Info>
