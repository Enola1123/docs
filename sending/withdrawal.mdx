Withdrawal lets you transfer USD or CNH from your YiPex fiat wallet directly to your registered company's own same-name bank account. Because the destination must be an account held in your company's name, YiPex does not require you to upload trade documentation when adding the account — making this the simplest path to repatriate funds to your business.

<Note>
YiPex provides its trade settlement services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities. Screenshots in this guide reflect test-environment data; always refer to the live product for exact details.
</Note>

<Tip>
Withdrawal accounts must be held in the name of your registered company. If you need to pay a third-party supplier or partner, use [Global Payment](/sending/global-payment) instead.
</Tip>

## Add a Withdrawal Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Recipient Management">
    Click **Personal Center** in the navigation menu, then select **Recipient Management** from the sub-menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new recipient">
    Click **New Recipient** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the account type">
    Choose **Withdrawal Account** on the New Recipient page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter account details">
    Follow the system prompts to enter your company bank account information. Confirm everything is correct, then click **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
YiPex does **not** review the withdrawal account immediately after submission. The review takes place when you initiate a payment to that account — not at setup.
</Note>

## Make a Withdrawal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment panel">
    Click **Pay** on the dashboard, or click **Transfer Out** beneath the relevant fiat currency balance in your fiat wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select wallet and method">
    Set **Payment Wallet** to **Fiat Wallet** and **Payment Method** to **Withdrawal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the withdrawal account">
    Use the drop-down to choose a saved withdrawal account. The collection currency is filled in automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Enter the **payment currency** and **payment amount**, then click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Enter your transaction password when prompted. If your super-admin has enabled Outgoing Review, also enter the review code sent to the super-admin email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment enters review">
    YiPex reviews the withdrawal before releasing funds. If additional AML documentation is needed, follow the system prompts to supply it. Track progress in **Recent Transactions** or **Fiat Wallet Ledger**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

YiPex sends an email notification once the withdrawal status updates. You can also check the status at any time under **Fiat Wallet Ledger**.

## Fixed Payment Amount vs Fixed Collection Amount

<Info>
The same Fixed Payment Amount / Fixed Collection Amount logic applies to cross-currency withdrawals. This distinction only matters when the payment currency differs from the collection currency.

**① FX Settlement (结汇)** — The fee is **included** in the amount you enter.
- Example (0.1% fee rate): enter 10,010.01 → fee deducted 10.01 → net amount settled 10,000.

**② Withdrawal** — The fee is **not included** in the amount you enter; it is deducted separately from your wallet balance.
- Example (0.1% fee rate): enter 10,000 → account receives 10,000 → fee of 10 deducted separately from your balance.

**Same-currency withdrawal:** Both options are identical — choose either.

**Cross-currency withdrawal:**
- **Fixed Payment Amount** — the amount you enter is in your payment currency.
- **Fixed Collection Amount** — the amount you enter is in your account's collection currency.
</Info>
