Your YiPex Crypto Wallet holds USDT and USDC received via blockchain. Every movement — whether an inbound deposit, a conversion, a stablecoin payment, or a transfer to your Fiat Wallet — is recorded in the Crypto Wallet Ledger, giving you a complete picture of your crypto balances at all times.

<Note>
  YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities. Screenshots in this guide reflect test-environment data; always refer to the live product for actual figures and features.
</Note>

## Viewing the Ledger

Navigate to **Crypto Wallet Ledger** to see a chronological list of all inflows (deposits and collections), conversions, and outflows (payments, express payout, and transfers to the Fiat Wallet). Click the **Details** button in the action column of any row to open the full transaction record.

## Transaction Detail Types

Each transaction type surfaces different information in its detail view.

### Inflow — Deposit or Collection

Beyond the basic amount, status, and timestamp, the detail page shows:

- **Sender's blockchain address** — click to copy it instantly.
- **Transaction hash** and **block height** confirming the on-chain record.
- A **"View in Block Explorer"** link that opens the transaction directly in a block explorer so you can independently verify the transfer.

### Conversion

The conversion detail page shows the transaction **status**, **time**, **executed amount**, and the **executed price** at which the conversion settled.

### Outflow — Transfer to Fiat Wallet

This detail page shows the **time**, **status**, and **amount** moved from the Crypto Wallet into the Fiat Wallet.

### Outflow — Stablecoin Payment or Transfer

Like an inflow detail, this page includes amount, status, and time, plus:

- **Recipient's blockchain address** — click to copy it instantly.
- **Transaction hash** and **block height**.
- A **"View in Block Explorer"** link for independent on-chain verification.

### Outflow — Express Payout

The express payout detail page shows the **time**, **status**, and **amount** of the payout.

<Tip>
  Use the **"View in Block Explorer"** link on any inflow or stablecoin payment detail page to independently verify the on-chain transaction without relying solely on YiPex records.
</Tip>
