YiPex supports real-time exchange between 21 fiat currencies directly within your Fiat Wallet. You can swap from any supported currency balance to another in just a few steps. The rate shown while you are entering amounts is a reference estimate only — the actual executed rate is locked in at the moment you confirm the exchange, not before.

<Note>
  YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities. Screenshots in this guide reflect test-environment data; always refer to the live product for actual figures and features.
</Note>

<Info>
  Exchange rates move in real time. The estimated buy amount displayed as you enter figures is a preview based on the current reference rate. Your actual settled amount is determined by the live rate at execution, which is locked when you click **Initiate Exchange** — not when you first enter your sell amount.
</Info>

## Steps to Exchange

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Exchange dialog">
    Click **Exchange** on the dashboard home page, or click **Exchange** under any specific fiat currency balance in your Fiat Wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the currency to sell">
    Choose the currency you want to sell from the dropdown. Your available balance for that currency is displayed automatically so you can see how much you have to work with.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the currency to buy">
    Choose the currency you want to receive. If you want to reverse the direction — for example, swap USD→CNH to CNH→USD — click the **swap button** (the two-arrow icon) between the two currency selectors to instantly flip the buy and sell sides.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the sell amount">
    Type in the amount you want to sell. YiPex automatically retrieves the current reference rate and displays an estimated buy amount. This figure is a preview only; the rate has not been locked yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request a live quote">
    Click **Request Quote** to fetch the latest live rate. A confirmation page opens showing the exact sell amount, the estimated buy amount, the currency pair, and the current rate — along with a countdown timer indicating how long that quoted rate remains valid.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and initiate the exchange">
    Review all details carefully — sell amount, buy amount, currencies, and rate. Click **Initiate Exchange** before the countdown timer expires to lock in the rate and complete the exchange. If the timer runs out before you confirm, request a new quote.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After initiating the exchange, track its status in **Recent Transactions** on the home page or in the **Fiat Wallet Ledger**.

<Warning>
  Once you click **Initiate Exchange**, the transaction cannot be reversed. Double-check the sell amount, buy amount, and both currency selections before you confirm.
</Warning>
