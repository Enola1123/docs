YiPex supports real-time exchange between 21 fiat currencies directly within your Fiat Wallet. You can swap from any supported currency balance to another in just a few steps. The rate shown while you are entering amounts is a reference only — the actual executed rate is locked at the moment you confirm the exchange.

<Info>
  Exchange rates are real-time and can move between the moment you enter an amount and the moment you confirm. The estimated buy amount displayed is a preview based on the current reference rate. Your actual settled amount is determined by the rate at execution, which is locked when you click **Initiate Exchange**.
</Info>

## Steps to Exchange

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Exchange dialog">
    Click **Exchange** on the dashboard home page, or click **Exchange** under any specific fiat currency balance in your Fiat Wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the currency to sell">
    Choose the currency you want to sell from the dropdown. Your available balance for that currency is displayed automatically so you can see how much you have to work with.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the currency to buy">
    Choose the currency you want to receive. If you want to reverse the direction — for example, swap USD→CNH to CNH→USD — click the **swap button** (the two-arrow icon) between the two currency selectors to instantly flip the buy and sell sides.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the sell amount">
    Type in the amount you want to sell. YiPex automatically retrieves the current reference rate and displays an estimated buy amount. This figure is a preview only and will be confirmed at the next step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request a live quote">
    Click **Request Quote** to fetch the latest live rate. A confirmation page opens showing the exact sell amount, the estimated buy amount, the currency pair, and the current rate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and initiate the exchange">
    Review all details carefully — sell amount, buy amount, currencies, and rate. A countdown timer is shown next to the quoted rate. Click **Initiate Exchange** before the timer expires to lock in the rate and complete the exchange.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After initiating the exchange, you can track its status in **Recent Transactions** on the home page or in the **Fiat Wallet Ledger**.

<Warning>
  Once you click **Initiate Exchange**, the transaction cannot be reversed. Double-check the sell amount, buy amount, and both currency selections before you confirm.
</Warning>

<Note>
  YiPex provides its global trade settlement services exclusively to entities that are not incorporated or resident in mainland China or the United States. Screenshots shown in this guide reflect YiPex test-environment data; always refer to the live product for actual figures and features.
</Note>
