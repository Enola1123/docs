Your YiPex Fiat Wallet holds USD, CNH, and other supported fiat currencies. The Fiat Wallet Ledger records every inflow — whether a fiat collection or a credit from your Crypto Wallet — as well as currency exchanges and outflows such as global payments and mainland China settlements. YiPex also performs AML reviews on inflows and outflows; this page is where you check review results, download payment receipts, and supply any additional materials requested.

## Viewing the Ledger

Navigate to **Fiat Wallet Ledger** to see all transactions with their current status and amounts. Click the **Details** link in the action column of any row to open the full record.

### Inflow — Fiat Collection

The detail page shows standard receipt information: amount received, currency, status, and relevant banking details from the paying party.

### Inflow — Fiat Wallet Credit

This record is created when funds arrive from a Crypto Wallet transfer. The detail page shows the conversion information — the crypto amount sold, the fiat amount credited, and the executed conversion details.

### Exchange

The exchange detail page shows the transaction **status**, **time**, the **amount bought**, the **amount sold**, and the **exchange rate** at which the trade executed.

### Outflow — Global Payment

The detail page shows the full payment information: payee details, payment currency, amount, and current processing status.

### Outflow — Mainland China Settlement

The detail page shows settlement-specific information including the settlement amount, destination account, and current status.

## Download Payment Receipt

For any outflow transaction, you can download an official payment receipt directly from the platform. Click the **Download Receipt** button in the action column of the outflow row, or click it from within the outflow detail page.

## Associate a Trade Order

After a fiat inflow lands in your wallet, you must associate it with a trade background document to satisfy compliance requirements. Find the inflow entry in the ledger and click **Associate Order**.

In the dialog that opens, select the matching trade order or orders that correspond to this inflow. Once you have made your selection, confirm the association.

<Note>
  Trade orders that are currently under review cannot be selected for association yet. Wait until the review is complete before linking those orders.
</Note>

## Supplement AML Information

YiPex conducts AML reviews on inflows and outflows. If additional supporting documents are required for a specific transaction, an **Add Supplement** button appears in the action column of that transaction row.

Click **Add Supplement** to open the supplementary materials dialog, then follow the on-screen prompts to upload the requested documents or provide the requested information. You can check the review outcome and any feedback directly in the Fiat Wallet Ledger.

<Note>
  YiPex provides its global trade settlement services exclusively to entities that are not incorporated or resident in mainland China or the United States. Screenshots shown in this guide reflect YiPex test-environment data; always refer to the live product for actual figures and features.
</Note>
