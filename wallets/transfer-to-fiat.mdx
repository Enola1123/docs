You can convert USDT or USDC from your Crypto Wallet into USD (or another supported fiat currency) and move it directly into your Fiat Wallet. This is useful when you want to pay international suppliers or settle in fiat after receiving stablecoin payments. The transfer is one-way: once funds leave the Crypto Wallet and enter the Fiat Wallet, they cannot be moved back.

<Warning>
  Transfers from the Crypto Wallet to the Fiat Wallet are one-way and cannot be reversed. Once a transfer is completed, the funds cannot be returned to the Crypto Wallet.
</Warning>

<Note>
  YiPex provides services exclusively to non-mainland-China and non-US entities. Screenshots in this guide reflect test-environment data; always refer to the live product for actual figures and features.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

Before initiating a transfer, make sure you have at least one approved trade order available to associate as AML/KYT supporting evidence for the incoming fiat funds. YiPex requires a trade order to be linked to every Fiat Wallet credit to satisfy compliance requirements.

## Steps to Transfer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the transfer dialog">
    Click **Transfer to Fiat Wallet** beneath your Crypto Wallet balance on the home page or Crypto Wallet section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the transfer amount">
    Type in the amount you want to convert and credit to your Fiat Wallet, then click **Convert**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Associate a trade order">
    A dialog opens prompting you to link a trade order as supporting documentation for this fiat credit. Select one or more trade orders. The combined total of all associated orders must equal the pending transfer amount exactly — YiPex will not allow you to proceed until the totals match precisely.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the transfer">
    Click **Confirm**. YiPex begins moving funds from your Crypto Wallet to your Fiat Wallet. While the transfer is being processed and reviewed, a **Pending Credit** entry appears in the Fiat Wallet Ledger so you can monitor its progress.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive confirmation">
    Once YiPex approves the transfer, you receive an email notification. Open the **Fiat Wallet Ledger** or check **Recent Transactions** on the home page to confirm that the credit has been applied to your Fiat Wallet balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  YiPex may request supplementary AML information during its review of the transfer. If this happens, a prompt appears on the pending transaction row in the Fiat Wallet Ledger. Follow the on-screen instructions to provide the requested materials promptly so the review is not delayed.
</Info>
